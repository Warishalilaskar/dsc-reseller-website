Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Digital Signature Certificates, e-Tokens, installation, renewal and troubleshooting.

What is a Digital Signature Certificate (DSC)? +
A Digital Signature Certificate (DSC) is an electronic certificate used to verify the identity of a person or organization in digital transactions. It can be used to digitally sign electronic documents and authenticate online submissions.
Why do I need a Digital Signature Certificate? +
A DSC helps establish your identity online and allows you to digitally sign documents securely. Depending on the applicable requirements, it may be used for income tax returns, e-tendering, company filings, GST-related activities and other online services.
What are the different classes or types of Digital Signature Certificates? +
Digital Signature Certificates are issued for different purposes and user categories. The type required depends on the service or portal where the certificate will be used. Check the relevant portal's current requirements before applying.
How can I apply for a Digital Signature Certificate? +
You can apply through an authorized Certifying Authority or its authorized partner. Complete the application, provide the required identity and verification details, and follow the applicable verification process.
What documents are required to apply for a DSC? +
The documents and verification requirements depend on the certificate type and applicant category. Applicants may need to provide identity details, contact information and other supporting documents requested during the application process.
How long is a Digital Signature Certificate valid? +
The validity period depends on the certificate issued. Check the validity dates shown in your certificate and arrange renewal before it expires, if continued use is required.
Can I renew my Digital Signature Certificate? +
Yes. Follow the renewal process provided by your Certifying Authority. You may need to complete identity verification and submit the information or documents requested for renewal.
What should I do if my DSC is not working? +
Check that your certificate is valid, your e-Token is connected correctly, and the required token drivers and signing software are installed. Try another USB port and follow the instructions provided by the relevant portal or service provider.
Can I use my DSC on different websites or portals? +
You may be able to use your DSC on multiple supported portals, provided the certificate type is accepted by each portal and the required software and configuration are available.
Is a Digital Signature Certificate legally valid? +
Digital signatures are legally recognized in India subject to the applicable laws, rules and requirements. Acceptance depends on the document, transaction and relevant authority.
What should I do if I forget my token password or PIN? +
Follow the instructions from your token manufacturer or service provider. Do not repeatedly guess the PIN, because the token may become locked. Some tokens require an administrator procedure or replacement if the PIN cannot be recovered.
How can I check whether my DSC is installed correctly? +
Connect your token and open the supported token management utility or certificate store. Confirm that the certificate is detected and valid, then follow the test procedure provided by the portal or signing application.
What should I do if my DSC has expired? +
An expired certificate generally cannot be used for new signing operations. Apply for renewal or a new certificate through your Certifying Authority and install or configure it according to the supplied instructions.
Where can I get help with my Digital Signature Certificate? +
Contact your DSC provider or Certifying Authority and provide the error message, operating system details, token model and the name of the portal where the issue occurs.
What is an e-Token? +
An e-Token is a hardware security device, usually connected through a USB port, that can securely store a Digital Signature Certificate and its associated cryptographic keys.
Why is an e-Token required for a Digital Signature Certificate? +
An e-Token helps protect the certificate and private key. When supported by the certificate and application, it allows the user to perform signing operations while keeping the private key protected by the device.
How do I install an e-Token? +
Connect the token to a USB port and install the correct driver or management software for your token model and operating system. Use the official installation instructions provided by the token manufacturer or service provider.
My computer does not detect the e-Token. What should I do? +

Try the following troubleshooting steps:

  • Disconnect the token and reconnect it securely.
  • Try a different USB port on your computer.
  • Check whether the correct token driver is installed.
  • Restart your computer and check the token again.
  • Test the token on another computer, if available.
  • If the issue continues, contact your token provider for assistance.
What should I do if the e-Token driver is not installing? +
Download the driver intended for your exact token model and operating system from an official source. Remove incompatible older drivers if the manufacturer's instructions recommend doing so, restart your computer, and try installing the correct driver again.
How can I change my e-Token PIN or password? +
Open the official token management utility and use its PIN-change option, if available. You will generally need the current PIN. Follow the token manufacturer's instructions and keep the new PIN secure.
What happens if I enter the wrong e-Token PIN repeatedly? +
Many tokens limit the number of incorrect PIN attempts. The token may become locked after too many failed attempts. Stop guessing the PIN and contact the token provider to check the supported recovery procedure.
Can I use the same e-Token on another computer? +
Usually, yes, if the computer supports the token and the necessary drivers, certificate utilities and signing software are installed. You may also need to configure the relevant browser or portal.
Can I use my e-Token on both Windows and macOS? +
Support depends on the token model, operating system version and availability of compatible drivers and signing software. Check the manufacturer's official compatibility information before using the token on another operating system.
What should I do if the e-Token is detected but my DSC is not visible? +
Open the token management utility and check whether the certificate is present. Confirm that the certificate has not expired and that the correct token and driver are being used. If the certificate is missing, contact your certificate provider.
How do I install a Digital Signature Certificate on an e-Token? +
The installation process depends on how the certificate was issued and the token model. Follow the instructions from your Certifying Authority or authorized provider. Never share your private key or token PIN with an untrusted person.
How do I safely remove an e-Token from my computer? +
Finish any signing operation and close applications that are using the token. Then remove it from the USB port. Avoid disconnecting the device while a signing or certificate operation is in progress.
Can I use one e-Token for multiple Digital Signature Certificates? +
This depends on the token's capacity, the certificate types and the issuing provider's requirements. Confirm compatibility and available storage with your provider before attempting to install additional certificates.
What should I do if my e-Token is physically damaged? +
Stop using the damaged device and contact the token provider or certificate issuer. Do not attempt to open or repair the token yourself. Ask about replacement options and the correct process for restoring access to your certificate.
Why does my browser or website not recognize the e-Token? +
Check the token driver, certificate status and the signing utility required by the website. Some portals require additional software or browser configuration. Follow the portal's official setup guide and check for any displayed error message.
How can I check whether my e-Token is working properly? +
Connect the token, open the official management utility and confirm that the device and certificate are detected. If available, use the provider's test procedure to verify that the token can perform the expected operations.
Does an e-Token expire? +
The hardware token and the Digital Signature Certificate have different lifecycles. A certificate may expire even if the token still works. Check the certificate's validity period and the token manufacturer's warranty or support terms.
How should I store and protect my e-Token? +
Keep the token in a safe, dry place and protect it from heat, moisture, dust and physical damage. Keep your PIN confidential, do not lend the token to unauthorized people, and remove it from the computer when it is not in use.
Where can I get technical support for my e-Token? +
Contact your token manufacturer, authorized reseller or Digital Signature Certificate provider. Share the token model, operating system and exact error message to help them diagnose the issue.
